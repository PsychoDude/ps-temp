Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

PSY Auth is a privately operated authentication service used to provide secure access to a limited collection of personal and internal applications.

This service is intended exclusively for the operator and authorized, invited users. It is not offered as a public or commercial service.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we may receive the following information from your Google account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google account identifier
  • Your profile picture (if available)

We may also collect basic authentication and security-related information, such as login timestamps, IP addresses, and authentication activity.

We do not request access to your Google password.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Google OAuth is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Grant access to authorized internal applications.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the service.
  • Investigate unauthorized access or technical issues.

Your information is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or commercial purposes.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Your authentication information may be shared with internal applications connected to this authentication service, solely for the purpose of providing authorized access and application functionality.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service.

5. Data Storage and Security

Authentication data is stored on privately managed infrastructure.

Reasonable technical measures are implemented to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

While we take precautions to protect your information, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to maintain your account, provide access to internal applications, and fulfill legitimate security requirements.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting the service operator.

Certain security logs or backup records may be retained temporarily before automatic expiration or deletion.

7. Google OAuth

Google authentication is provided through Google's OAuth services. We only request the Google account information necessary to identify and authenticate users.

Your use of Google's authentication services is also subject to Google's own Privacy Policy.

You may revoke this application's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account settings .

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the service or its operation. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
